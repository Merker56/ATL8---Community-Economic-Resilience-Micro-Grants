1. Executive Summary & Official Judging Rubric Alignment

Municipal grant officers allocate scarce micro-grant stabilization pools (typically $50,000 per corridor) under tight committee deadlines. Naive spreadsheets and raw graph degree centrality fail because they accidentally prioritize virtual mailbox agglomerations (140 shell LLCs at 2261 Market St), single-industry BEA concordance monopolies (Primo), or redundant grantees whose 2-hop downstream buyer subgraphs overlap by 90–100%.

Our platform pairs a live BigQuery Property Graph (a4i_econ.LocalEconomy), a Google ADK Multi-Agent System (root_agent + 3 specialized sub-agents), a Managed BigQuery MCP Toolset, and a Dual-Console UI to generate an audit-ready 7-Part Friday Committee Defense Memo and a Subgraph-Deduplicated Grant Portfolio.

Sub-Agent 1: graph_cascade_analyst

Executes 1-to-2 hop ANY SHORTEST GQL supply cascades, serializes TO_JSON(p) paths with lat/lon coordinates, filters candidates into the 5–25 2-Hop Sweet-Spot Band, and simulates Track 3 Bi-Directional Anchor Closure Shocks (Block 060750207003000, 785 jobs → 106 storefronts → 36 upstream suppliers hurt).

Sub-Agent 2: equity_bias_auditor

Runs the 3-Step Equity & BEA Mechanical Bias Audit: verifies 11-char ACS tracts with 0 race/gender/age inputs, flags BEA's 78% food/mfg concentration vs. 0% personal services leaf nodes (recommending a 15% non-graph direct-relief reserve), penalizes virtual mailbox hubs (0.55x), and records +10 Bonus Merchant Supplier Intake edges.

Sub-Agent 3: grant_portfolio_defender

Synthesizes the 7-Part Friday Committee Defense Memo: proves why Sweet-Spot Keystone Theatre Rhinoceros (14.69 cascading jobs saved, $1,701.84/job) beats 38-edge Hub Trap Cafe Du Nord (9.55 jobs saved, $2,618.23/job), and optimizes the $50,000 portfolio into Theatre Rhinoceros + Canela (21 unique 2-hop nodes, 13 physical storefronts, 27.80 jobs saved).

Managed BigQuery MCP (agent/tools.yaml)

Defines 5 parameterized bigquery-sql MCP tools (mcp_discover_sweet_spot_candidates, mcp_trace_2hop_supply_cascade, mcp_simulate_anchor_closure_shock, mcp_audit_corridor_equity_and_bias, mcp_verify_merchant_supplier_edge) bound to qwiklabs-gcp-03-46ba9aeed89b.a4i_econ.LocalEconomy.
